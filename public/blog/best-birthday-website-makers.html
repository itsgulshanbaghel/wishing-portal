1. TheGreeter — Most Versatile & Value-Packed Personal Experience

TheGreeter is built specifically for expressive one-to-one surprise links rather than corporate group boards. Beyond birthdays, you get tailored layouts for girlfriends, boyfriends, moms, best friends, 30th milestones, interactive proposals, and native Hindi birthday wishes.

Where it stands out: You can preview your full site for free first, publish from just ₹59 (Starter) or ₹99 (Pro with custom vanity URL like thegreeter.in/demo-raman, printable QR code, and virtual blow-candle cake), lock the entrance behind a 3–6 digit PIN keypad, add custom music or a personal voice note, and update typos later using your Creator Edit PIN. You can even choose a ₹299 Infinity Lifetime plan so the memories stay online forever.

2. OpenMe — Focused Single-Experience Birthday Gift

OpenMe is a clean Indian birthday website maker offering one polished card flow combining photos, a Spotify track, a written letter, and cake cutting locked behind a PIN for a flat ₹199 one-time payment. If you specifically want Spotify integration and a single streamlined birthday layout, it is a solid contender—though it lacks TheGreeter's lower ₹59/₹99 entry tiers, custom uploaded voice notes, Hindi bilingual templates, lifetime hosting option, and multi-occasion maker (anniversaries, proposals, apologies, festivals).

3. Kudoboard — Best for 20+ Coworker Messages

If HR or a big team needs 30 colleagues to drop a quick note and GIF onto one shared wall, Kudoboard shines. However, for a partner, parent, or close friend, a Pinterest-style grid of office notes lacks intimacy, music storytelling, and interactive moments like blowing out virtual candles together.

4. Canva & Paperless Post — Best for Print & Party RSVPs

Canva excels when you want a printable PDF or WhatsApp image poster, and Paperless Post handles formal dinner-party RSVP tracking cleanly. Neither delivers a mobile-first interactive webpage with polaroid timelines, scratch reveals, midnight countdowns, or an ad-free celebratory flow.
