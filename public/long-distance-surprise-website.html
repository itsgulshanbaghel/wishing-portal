Zero Shipping Delays. 100% Personal Emotion.

Sending physical gifts internationally or last-minute across cities often means customs delays, generic packaging, and missing the exact midnight moment. A personalized digital surprise opens instantaneously anywhere in the world.

Even better, you can combine interactive music, recorded voice notes, and shared photos that capture your unique inside story far better than a store-bought card.

Long-Distance Ideas That Always Hit Home

  • FaceTime Reaction Drop: Share the link during your nightly video call and watch their face light up live.
  • Countdown to Next Reunion: Add a heartfelt message counting down the days until your next airport hug.
  • Screenshot Collage: Include funny or sleepy screenshots from your favorite late-night calls.
  • Just-Because Pick-Me-Up: Send a Gratitude or Apology page on a hard work or exam week when they miss you most.

Send Your Long-Distance Surprise Today

  1. Choose Anniversary, Birthday, Proposal, or Sorry in the Creator Studio.
