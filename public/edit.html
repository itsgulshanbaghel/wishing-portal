Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my website link or ID?
Your website ID was generated when you created your page (e.g. gllqs6we69). You can find your full link in your WhatsApp or SMS chat history where you shared it, your browser history, or your payment confirmation.
Can I change photos, music, or messages after publishing?
Yes! When you open your website in the editor, all existing photos, audio, secret notes, and messages are automatically loaded. You can delete or replace photos, upload new background music, and change any text.
Do I need to pay again when saving edits?
No! If your website already has a paid premium package, all re-edits are 100% free. The editor will save your changes directly to the cloud without opening the payment gateway.
Will my link or QR code change after editing?
No, your link, custom vanity URL (e.g. thegreeter.in/your-name), and QR code remain permanently unchanged. Anyone who visits your link will immediately see your latest updates.
