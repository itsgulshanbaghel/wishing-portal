Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plans, billing, and surprise websites.

Is this a recurring subscription or a one-time payment?
It is 100% a one-time payment! There are no auto-renewals, recurring monthly charges, or hidden fees. You only pay once for the duration you choose.
Can I edit photos or messages after making a payment?
Yes! During creation, you set a 4–6 digit secret Edit PIN. Using this PIN, you can log in to edit text, swap photos, change music, and update messages as many times as you like for free.
How does the custom vanity URL work?
With Pro, Pro+, and Infinity plans, you can choose a personalized URL such as thegreeter.in/HappyBirthdayAarav. You will also receive an automatically generated custom QR code ready to print or share.
What payment methods are supported?
For users in India, we support UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit and Credit cards via Cashfree Payments. For international users, we support Credit/Debit cards and PayPal.
How quickly is my website live after payment?
Instantly! As soon as payment is confirmed, your surprise website is live on high-speed global servers. You receive your shareable link and QR code immediately on screen.
What happens when the plan duration expires?
When a standard duration plan ends, the website link will gracefully expire. If you wish to keep your surprise page online forever, we recommend choosing the Infinity (Lifetime) plan.
