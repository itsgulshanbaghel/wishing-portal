Make Turning 30 Feel Iconic

A 30th birthday is one of life’s biggest milestones — a moment friends and partners want to make truly memorable. A personalized digital surprise website pulls together decades of memories into one interactive keepsake link.

Whether you are hosting a surprise party (and printing the QR code on invitations) or celebrating long-distance, it brings everyone together.

Ideas for a 30th Milestone Page

  • 30 Reasons We Love You: Use the message section and pop-up balloons to list fun memories and milestones.
  • The Evolution Gallery: Pick one photo from age 5, 15, 21, 25, and 30 for an unforgettable visual glow-up.
  • Party QR Code Reveal: Place the custom QR code inside a birthday card or on the cake box.
  • Midnight Countdown: Send the countdown link on the final evening of their 20s!

How to Build a 30th Birthday Website

  1. Open the Creator Studio, select Birthday, and enter "30" in the Age field.
  2. Choose Happy, Funny, or Roast mood for milestone-ready pre-written messages.
  3. Upload throwback photos, pick a soundtrack, and customize every section live.
  4. Share the custom link in your friend group or print the QR code for the party!
