Turn Scattered Photos Into a Story They Can Scroll Through

Posting a quick Instagram story collage disappears in 24 hours. A birthday card with photos on TheGreeter curates your shared history into dedicated sections — a memory timeline, captioned polaroid frames, and interactive image reveals paired with your own words and music.

Every upload works right from your phone gallery with zero formatting stress. You can rearrange captions, test multiple birthday templates, and preview the exact mobile look free.

Photo Curation Prompts That Always Work

  • 1st Photo — Iconic Duo Shot: Open strong with your favorite happy picture together.
  • Middle Photos — Embarrassing Throwbacks: Mix in two funny candid moments or old screenshots for instant laughs.
  • Short Specific Captions: Write tiny prompts under each polaroid like "Goa rainstorm, 2024" or "2 AM Maggi run".
  • Final Photo — Toast to Next Year: End with a recent portrait alongside your main birthday wish.

How to Build Your Photo Birthday Card

  1. Start by entering their name above and choosing Birthday in the Creator Studio.
  2. Pick any photo-rich birthday template inside the interactive previewer.
  3. Enable Polaroid, Memory Timeline, or Instant Camera sections and upload up to 9 photos directly from your device.
  4. Add captions, pair background music, preview live, and generate your shareable URL.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos can I add to a birthday website?
You can add up to 9 curated memory photos across the timeline, polaroid, and interactive photo reveal sections, with custom captions on each memory.
Are my uploaded photos private?
Yes! Generated user pages are unlisted, excluded in robots.txt, and served with noindex headers so only people with your shared link or QR code can open them.
Can I replace or add more photos later?
Yes, every published page includes a secret Edit PIN so you can swap or update photos anytime at thegreeter.in/edit without changing your URL.
